How to fly a mission

  1. Design. Pick a destination, rocket, spacecraft, engine, antenna, orbit and instruments. Stay inside the budget and the rocket’s mass limit.
  2. Launch. The rocket and the orbit insertion can fail, so reliability matters.
  3. Fly. Each turn, choose Observe, Downlink or Recharge. Handle surprises before the timer runs out.
  4. Score. Only data sent to Earth counts. Reach the mission objective for a bonus.
Quick glossary

Delta-v: the total change in speed a spacecraft needs to reach its target.
Isp: how efficiently an engine uses propellant. Higher means less fuel for the same job.
Downlink: sending data to Earth. Data only scores once it arrives.
Brownout: the battery runs out and the craft drops into a low-power safe mode.
Reserve: unspent budget kept for emergency fixes and upgrades.
